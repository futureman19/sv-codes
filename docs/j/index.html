WHAT JUST HAPPENED

① Money came out of a sticker

A Bitcoin transaction moved from the physical world onto your phone. No signup, no app store, no ID check. That's what Bitcoin was always supposed to feel like.

② The colored grid is for machines

QR codes were built for humans in 1994. SV Codes are built for machines: any camera can read them at any angle, they carry whole Bitcoin transactions, and they work with zero internet — the message travels as light.

③ Anyone can verify it

Every SV Code is cryptographically signed. When your phone decoded the grid above, it checked the signature itself — no server told it what to trust. That's the protocol: sv-codes, open and free for anyone to build on.
